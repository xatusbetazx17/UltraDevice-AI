# Glossary

- **SoC:** battery state of charge, represented as a fraction from zero to one.
- **Wh:** energy; watts multiplied by hours.
- **Duty:** fraction of time or modeled activity assigned to a peripheral.
- **Boost:** time-limited high-activity mode; no physical transformation.
- **Harvest:** explicitly supplied energy from environmental inputs.
- **External power:** a specified additional source; never assumed free energy.
- **Unserved energy:** requested load the model could not deliver.
- **RC thermal model:** a single thermal resistance and thermal capacitance approximation.
- **Unknown:** a missing measurement represented by null, not zero or an estimate.
- **Hash-linked log:** edit-detecting local records, not authenticated immutable storage.
